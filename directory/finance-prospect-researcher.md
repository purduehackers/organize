# [Finance] Prospect/Lead Researcher

Purdue Hackers works with companies that care about ambitious student builders.

Some companies want to recruit great engineers. Some want to support creative technical projects on campus. Some want to get their tools in the hands of students who will actually use them. Some have never heard of Purdue Hackers before, but would immediately understand why this community matters if the right person found them and framed the opportunity well.

We’re looking for a Prospect Researcher to help the finance team find those companies.

This role is about researching potential sponsors, recruiting partners, alumni connections, startup friends, and other organizations we should build relationships with. You’ll help identify companies that are a strong fit for Purdue Hackers, figure out who we should talk to, understand what they might care about, and prepare the context our outreach team needs to start a real conversation.

This is a great role for someone who is curious, thorough, and excited by the strategy side of fundraising. You are not just making a spreadsheet of random companies. You are helping build the pipeline that funds Hack Night, Microgrants, The Show, workshops, hardware projects, student experiments, and everything else Purdue Hackers does.

## What you will do

- Research companies that may be a good fit for Purdue Hackers sponsorship, recruiting, or partnership programs
- Identify relevant contacts at companies, including recruiters, founders, engineers, developer relations teams, university recruiting teams, and alumni
- Evaluate prospects based on how well they fit Purdue Hackers’ community, programs, audience, and goals
- Maintain and improve our prospect database/CRM with useful notes, contact info, relationship status, and next steps
- Help prioritize which companies the finance team should reach out to first
- Prepare short research briefs so outreach feels specific, informed, and compelling
- Look for warm paths through alumni, organizers, community members, sponsors, and existing relationships
- Work with the broader finance team to turn research into real sponsorship and recruiting opportunities

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Are curious about companies, startups, recruiting, sponsorships, and how organizations make decisions
- Are good at researching online and finding useful information quickly
- Are organized and comfortable keeping track of lots of prospects, notes, and moving pieces
- Have good judgment about which companies are worth pursuing and why
- Can think strategically about what a company might want from a relationship with Purdue Hackers
- Like making systems clearer, cleaner, and easier for other people to act on
- Are excited by the idea that good research can directly turn into funding, opportunities, and resources for students

Bonus points if you:

- Have experience with sales, fundraising, recruiting, partnerships, CRM tools, or startup research
- Are familiar with tech companies, developer tools, AI startups, hardware companies, or university recruiting
- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
