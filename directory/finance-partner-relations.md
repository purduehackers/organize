# [Finance] Partner Relations

Purdue Hackers has a lot of people in our corner. Sponsors who have supported Hack Night. Companies who have visited campus. Alumni who want to help. Founders, engineers, recruiters, faculty, and friends of the community who believe Purdue should have a stronger builder culture.

We’re looking for a Partner Relations lead to help take care of those relationships.

This role is about working with companies and people we’re already warm with: understanding what they care about, keeping them updated on what Purdue Hackers is doing, helping them have a great experience when they come to campus, and finding ways for the relationship to grow over time.

A good partner relationship should not feel transactional. It should feel like both sides understand each other. Companies should know what Purdue Hackers is building, where they can be helpful, and why this community is special. Students should get access to better events, resources, opportunities, and people because those relationships are strong.

You’ll work closely with the finance team, the storyteller, and event leads. You’ll also help shape our VIP newsletter, which keeps sponsors, alumni, faculty, and friends of Purdue Hackers updated on what we’ve been building.

## What you will do

- Manage relationships with existing sponsors, warm company contacts, alumni, and friends of Purdue Hackers
- Understand what partners care about and identify ways Purdue Hackers can create value for them
- Help companies plan campus visits, tech talks, AMAs, workshops, recruiting events, and other activations
- Coordinate logistics when partners come to campus, including schedules, rooms, promotion, attendees, and follow-up
- Keep partners updated on Purdue Hackers programs, events, student projects, and sponsorship opportunities
- Work closely with the storyteller to write and shape the VIP newsletter
- Help maintain our CRM with notes, relationship history, next steps, and partner preferences
- Look for ways to turn warm relationships into deeper support for Hack Night, Microgrants, The Show, workshops, and other programs

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Are personable, thoughtful, and good at making people feel taken care of
- Like building long-term relationships instead of just making one-time asks
- Can understand what a company, sponsor, alum, or recruiter might need from Purdue Hackers
- Are organized enough to manage follow-ups, logistics, notes, and moving pieces
- Have strong judgment around tone, professionalism, and communication
- Enjoy connecting people to opportunities, resources, and each other
- Are excited by the idea of helping Purdue Hackers build a real network of supporters around the community

Bonus points if you:

- Are comfortable writing updates, emails, recaps, or relationship notes
- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
