# [Design] Designer (General)

Purdue Hackers design is not one thing.

It can be a poster, a badge, a website, a slide deck, a sticker, a table setup, a motion graphic, a printed artifact, a visual system for a one-night event, or an entire brand exploration for something hundreds of people will experience.

We’re looking for Designers who want to experiment across all of it.

This role is intentionally broad. Purdue Hackers runs a lot of different programs, and each one needs a different kind of visual language. Some weeks, you might be designing a quick asset for an announcement. Other weeks, you might be building the entire identity for a major event from scratch: the name, the concept, the typography, the colors, the posters, the website, the entire experience, and the little details that make the whole thing come together.

Every semester, we run events that create room for full end-to-end brand exploration. These are chances for you to take a raw idea and turn it into a complete visual world. You’ll have real ownership over the direction, not just execution. If you have a weird design idea you’ve always wanted to try, Purdue Hackers is probably one of the few places on campus where you can actually make it happen.

This is a role for someone who wants range: polished systems, strange experiments, physical objects, digital graphics, event identities, and everything in between. The goal is not just to make Purdue Hackers look good. The goal is to make the things we do feel specific, memorable, and alive.

## What you will do

- Design visual assets for Purdue Hackers events, programs, announcements, and campaigns
- Help shape branding for Purdue Hackers, Hack Night, The Show, and other events
- Create posters, social graphics, slide decks, printed materials, signage, and other promotional assets
- Work on creative artifacts like badges, merch, stickers, table materials, installation pieces, and other weird physical/digital objects
- Help maintain and evolve Purdue Hackers’ core visual identity across mediums
- Collaborate with organizers, storytellers, engineers, and event leads to make their ideas look and feel real
- Build reusable design systems, templates, and assets so future organizers can move faster
- Bring taste, polish, and weirdness to the small details that make Purdue Hackers feel special

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Deeply resonate with Purdue Hackers and want to help make it feel more magical
- Have strong visual taste and care about typography, layout, color, spacing, and detail
- Enjoy designing across different formats, not just one medium
- Are excited by both polished brand systems and strange one-off creative experiments
- Can take a vague idea and turn it into something visually compelling
- Like working with other people to understand what they are trying to communicate
- Are willing to move quickly, iterate, and ship things even when they are not perfect
- Care about making Purdue Hackers feel distinct, memorable, and alive

Bonus points if you:

- Have experience with Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, Blender, After Effects, or other design tools
- Have made posters, merch, websites, event branding, motion graphics, or physical design objects before
- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
