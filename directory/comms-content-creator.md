# [Comms] Content Creator

Purdue Hackers is a home for students who make creative technical projects together. We run Hack Night every Friday, help students get funding and equipment for ambitious ideas, and create spaces where weird, curious, technical people can find their people.

A huge part of making that work is showing the world what is happening here!

We’re looking for a Content Creator to help capture the energy of Purdue Hackers and turn it into posts, photos, videos, and stories that make people want to show up. This role is about documenting the people, projects, chaos, late-night builds, demos, inside jokes, and tiny magical moments that make Purdue Hackers feel alive.

This is not just “make Instagram posts for events”. Good content helps students realize Purdue Hackers is for them. It helps sponsors, alumni, and faculty understand why this community matters. It helps us build a world that people want to be part of.

## What you will do

- Capture photos and videos at Hack Night, callouts, workshops, and other Purdue Hackers events
- Create short-form content for Instagram, YouTube, Twitter, and other channels
- Interview members about their projects, stories, and experiences in Purdue Hackers
- Turn raw moments from the community into polished posts, reels, carousels, and other content
- Help promote upcoming events in a way that feels exciting, authentic, and very Purdue Hackers
- Work with the comms/design team to shape the public voice and visual identity of Purdue Hackers
- Build a reusable content pipeline so we are regularly documenting what people are making

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Like taking photos, filming videos, editing clips, writing captions, or making internet-native content
- Are comfortable talking to people and asking them about what they’re building
- Have strong taste and care about making things feel polished, thoughtful, and alive
- Are excited by the idea of helping define how Purdue Hackers appears to the outside world

Bonus points if you:

- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
