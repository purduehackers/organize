# About Purdue Hackers

Making creative technical projects is highly rewarding—but academic and social obligations make it hard to find the time & energy to do it regularly. Purdue Hackers aims to help others find their people and make things together regularly.

Please read the `README.md` for more information if you haven't already.

# About the role

Purdue Hackers does a lot of cool things, and we need people to help make sure people actually hear about them.

We’re looking for Comms Producers to work closely with the Comms Lead and the rest of the team to help create and publish content across blogs and social media. This role is hands-on and collaborative, focused on making things real.

## What you will do

- Brainstorm ideas for social posts, blog content, and small content experiments
- Create and post content on Twitter/X and Instagram
- Work closely with the comms & design team to bring ideas to life
- Collaborate with community members to help share their projects, ideas, and stories (through blog posts, reels--whatever you come up with!)
- Help write announcements, short updates, or event recaps when needed
- Make sure members’ work is seen and celebrated

While your main focus will be PR related work, you are encouraged to wear many hats & contribute to any other part of Purdue Hackers if you feel inspired to do so.

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Are extraordinarly kind, humble, creative, and curious
- Deeply resonate with Purdue Hackers & feel inspired to make it better
- Enjoy collaborating with others and working as part of a team
- Like writing, posting, or coming up with content ideas
- Have a good sense of tone and taste online

Bonus points if you:

- Have written blogs, posts, or announcements before
- Are active on social media and understand how it works
- Enjoy keeping up with what’s happening in a community
- Are interested in creative tech or storytelling

BEING A CS MAJOR IS NOT A REQUIREMENT! We are looking for people from all backgrounds to join our team!! Folks who help Purdue Hackers out range from the College of Science to Engineering to Liberal Arts and Polytechnic!

## Time commitment

Some organizers spend 5-10 hours per week; others spend 20-30 hours per week. Some weeks will be heavier than others, but you can probably send somewhere in the 5-10 hour range and be successful. We're all full-time students, though, & are happy to be flexible.

# How to apply

Send an email to phackers@purdue.edu including **"comms producer spring 2026”** (not case-sensitive) in the subject line with the following:

- Tell us about yourself. Who are you, what are you studying, what are some of your hobbies, any other relevant things?
- What resonates with you about Purdue Hackers?
- What resonates with you about this role?
- If you have a portfolio of past work, please share it. (It's ok if you don't)
- Anything else you find relevant

The structure of the email is up to you, but please "be real"—we're students reading these too, and we're strongly anti-bullshit. Be authentically yourself!

## Next steps

We will reply to confirm we've received it; after that, you will only hear from us if we're interested in following up. There's no deadline on either end, and it's possible this position won't be filled for a while.

If we're interested in following up, we'll reach out via email to chat some more. You may be asked to come to a Hack Night (Fridays after 8pm at the Bechtel Center) to meet the team and see how we run things, and get some live situational experience!

After the meeting, we will follow up with a response—acceptance or rejection—within a week.
