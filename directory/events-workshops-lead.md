# [Events] Workshops Lead

Purdue Hackers workshops are where people get to teach the things they are obsessed with.

We’ve run workshops in the past year alone on GPU programming, compression, animations in Rive, AI coding agents, hardware, creative coding, peer-to-peer infrastructure, or other really cool topics one of our community members are super passionate aboput.

We’re looking for a Workshops Lead to help make more of that happen.

This role is about finding people in the Purdue Hackers community who are excited to teach something, helping them shape that idea into a real session, and owning the end-to-end process of making the workshop happen. You’ll help coordinate logistics, marketing, materials, room setup, RSVPs, reminders, and the many tiny details that result in a packed room of people learning together.

The process is already heavily documented, but there is a lot of room to improve it. You’ll inherit a system that works, then get to make it smoother, more ambitious, and more repeatable.

This is a great role for someone who likes bringing people together, making things happen, and helping others share what they love with a large audience.

## What you will do

- Find community members who are interested in teaching workshops on topics they care about
- Help workshop hosts turn rough ideas into clear, exciting sessions
- Own the end-to-end workshop process: planning, scheduling, marketing, logistics, setup, and follow-up
- Coordinate with designers, storytellers, and organizers to promote workshops across Discord, Instagram, email, and other channels
- Make sure hosts have what they need: room, equipment, slides, examples, links, supplies, and support
- Attend workshops to help with setup, check-in, troubleshooting, and making the room feel welcoming
- Improve our existing workshop systems, documentation, templates, and playbooks
- Build a culture where more Purdue Hackers feel excited and confident teaching what they know

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Are organized enough to manage logistics, deadlines, reminders, and moving pieces
- Enjoy talking to people and learning what they are excited about
- Are good at turning vague ideas into concrete plans
- Care about making educational events feel fun, welcoming, and worth showing up to
- Are willing to follow an existing process, but also notice where it can be improved
- Like the idea of helping someone teach their first workshop to a room full of people
- Are excited by technical, creative, and weird topics, even when you are not already an expert in them

Bonus points if you:

- Have taught something before, formally or informally
- Have organized events, workshops, classes, clubs, or community programs before
- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
