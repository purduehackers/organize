# Read this before applying

Purdue Hackers is a group of students who help each other build creative technical projects. We're always looking for a few new organizers to join our team. Thanks for checking out our roles page! :D

## What is Purdue Hackers?

Building creative technical projects is incredibly rewarding, but it can be hard to find the time, energy, and community to do it consistently alongside classes, work, and everything else.

Purdue Hackers exists to give students the time, space, and community to make ambitious things together.

We run three core programs:

- **Hack Night**: Our flagship program: a hacker festival every Friday night at the Bechtel Center.
- **Microgrants**: Small grants that help students kickstart ambitious project ideas.
- **The Show**: Our annual demo day, where members share what they've built throughout the year with hundreds of students, faculty, sponsors, and guests. Past examples include: [spill ≋ (2025)](https://spill.purduehackers.com), [BURST ✷ (2024)](https://burst.purduehackers.com)

Behind the scenes, Purdue Hackers also has day-to-day operations across engineering, communications, design, events, finance, and more. These teams keep the organization running and help us support builders across campus.

We operate a lot like a startup: projects have directly responsible individuals, deliverables, and deadlines. We think of our work as "shipping" for the students we serve. [Learn more about how Purdue Hackers operates internally and why we think it's so great.](https://blog.purduehackers.com/posts/college-clubs-are-awesome)
