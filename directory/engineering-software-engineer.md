# [Engineering] Software Engineer

Purdue Hackers runs on software. The Hack Night dashboard, passports, events tooling, server infrastructure, monitoring, and all the glue that keeps our weird ecosystem alive.

We’re looking for Software Engineers to help build and maintain the technical systems behind Purdue Hackers. This role is for people who want to build real software for a real community, with real users, real constraints, and plenty of room to make things weird.

You might work on the Hack Night dashboard, our doohickey coordination API, passports infrastructure, internal tools, websites, server infrastructure, or entirely new systems we have not thought of yet. Some projects will feel like normal product engineering. Others will feel like building infrastructure for a tiny fictional country. You’ll have very high ownership over what you want to build and the direction you take it.

Purdue Hackers has a growing universe of software, hardware, events, rituals, and community systems that all need to talk to each other. We need people who want to own parts of that universe end-to-end: design the system, build it, ship it, maintain it, and make it better over time!

## What you will do

- Build and maintain software for Purdue Hackers events, programs, and internal operations
- Work on systems like the Hack Night dashboard, doohickey coordination API, Passports infrastructure, and event check-in flows
- Help manage our servers, infrastructure, deployments, databases, APIs, and monitoring
- Design tools that make Hack Night, Microgrants, The Show, and other programs easier to run
- Build integrations between our websites, hardware projects, internal tools, and community systems
- Collaborate with designers, hardware engineers, organizers, and storytellers to turn ideas into real products
- Improve reliability, security, performance, and developer experience across Purdue Hackers software
- Document systems so future organizers can understand, maintain, and build on them

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Enjoy building software that people actually use
- Are comfortable programming and willing to learn whatever tools are needed for a project
- Like owning projects end-to-end, from idea to architecture to implementation to maintenance
- Care about making systems reliable, understandable, and easy for future people to work on
- Are excited by the idea of building software that touches real-world events, hardware, and community rituals
- Can debug weird problems, ask for help, and keep going when the codebase is super cursed
- Want to help make Purdue Hackers feel more like a living, hackable ecosystem

Bonus points if you:

- Have experience with TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node, SQLite, APIs, Linux servers, Docker, auth, networking, or infrastructure
- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
