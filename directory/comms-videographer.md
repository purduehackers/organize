# [Comms] Videographer

Purdue Hackers is full of moments that are hard to describe unless you were there.

A founder gives an AMA and says something that changes how a student thinks about their career. A workshop turns into a room full of people building their first coding agent. Someone demos a project at midnight and the room erupts. A sponsor visits campus and realizes Purdue has way more hackers than they expected.

We want to capture more of that.

We’re looking for a Videographer to help film Purdue Hackers events, sessions, workshops, AMAs, demos, and other things we run throughout the year. This role is about documenting the energy of the room and turning it into content that helps more people understand what Purdue Hackers is.

You’ll help us record talks, capture event footage, make recap videos, and build a library of high-quality footage we can use for social media, sponsor updates, newsletters, and future storytelling. Some of this will be polished. Some of it will be scrappy. All of it will help preserve the work, people, and culture that make Purdue Hackers special.

## What you will do

- Film Purdue Hackers events, including Hack Nights, workshops, AMAs, tech talks, callouts, demo days, and sponsor sessions
- Record high-quality footage of speakers, attendees, demos, projects, and the general energy of the room
- Help create recap videos, short-form clips, speaker highlights, and behind-the-scenes content
- Work with the content, comms, and storytelling teams to turn raw footage into useful public-facing material
- Help document major Purdue Hackers programs like Hack Night, Microgrants, and The Show
- Organize footage so future organizers can easily find and reuse it
- Help develop a consistent visual style for how Purdue Hackers appears in video

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Enjoy filming events, people, talks, projects, or behind-the-scenes moments
- Have a good eye for composition, lighting, sound, pacing, and energy
- Are comfortable moving around events and capturing footage without disrupting the room
- Can notice small moments that make a community feel alive
- Are organized enough to manage footage, files, and editing handoff
- Are excited by the idea of helping define how Purdue Hackers is remembered visually

Bonus points if you:

- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
