# About Purdue Hackers

Making creative technical projects is highly rewarding—but academic and social obligations make it hard to find the time & energy to do it regularly. Purdue Hackers aims to help others find their people and make things together regularly.

Please read the `README.md` for more information if you haven't already.

# About the Role

At its core, Purdue Hackers is a creative-technical organization, and we on the Engineering team deal with both halves of that! Engineering creates the technical projects that keep Purdue Hackers running, like the Hack Night dashboard, our Discord bot Wack Hacker, our Passports initiative, maintaining our websites, and many, many other core functions. In addition, we also work on projects including The Sign, purely creative endeavors that keep the spirit of Purdue Hackers alive and well. Soul goes into everything we do, and as an Engineer you would be directly contributing to this noble cause.

## What You'll Do

- Either bring your own project idea or take up a preexisting project looking for help
- Work towards milestones with consistent contributions and updates
- Work closely with the other members of Engineering to fix issues with both your and other people's projects
- Collaborate with other teams on large-scale projects that set the direction and identity of Purdue Hackers

## About You

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Are extraordinarly kind, humble, creative, and curious
- Deeply resonate with Purdue Hackers & feel inspired to make it better
- Are inspired to forge your own unique path to contribute to Purdue Hackers' technical projects and infrastructure
- Love to collaborate with others to make big ideas and projects come to life

Bonus points if you:

- Have experience with making your own creative-technical projects
- Already have creative-technical ideas for how to make Hack Night or any of our other endeavors even more magical
- Love to know what's going on around you and contribute where you feel comfortable

BEING A CS MAJOR IS NOT A REQUIREMENT! We are looking for people from all backgrounds to join our team!! Folks who help Purdue Hackers out range from the College of Science to Engineering to Liberal Arts and Polytechnic! Everyone on the Engineering team wants to make sure you're making real contributions to our mission and are always willing to lend a hand to make that happen.

## Time commitment

Some organizers spend 5-10 hours per week; others spend 20-30 hours per week. Some weeks will be heavier than others, but you can probably send somewhere in the 5-10 hour range and be successful. We're all full-time students, though, & are happy to be flexible.

# How to apply

Send an email to phackers@purdue.edu including **"engineering spring 2026”** (not case-sensitive) in the subject line with the following:

- Tell us about yourself. Who are you, what are you studying, what are some of your hobbies, any other relevant things?
- What resonates with you about Purdue Hackers?
- What resonates with you about this role?
- If you have a portfolio of past work, please share it. (It's ok if you don't)
- Anything else you find relevant

The structure of the email is up to you, but please "be real"—we're students reading these too, and we're strongly anti-bullshit. Be authentically yourself!

## Next steps

We will reply to confirm we've received it; after that, you will only hear from us if we're interested in following up. There's no deadline on either end, and it's possible this position won't be filled for a while.

If we're interested in following up, we'll reach out via email to chat some more. You may be asked to come to a Hack Night (Fridays after 8pm at the Bechtel Center) to meet the team and see how we run things, and get some live situational experience!

After the meeting, we will follow up with a response—acceptance or rejection—within a week.
