# [Finance] Sponsor Outreach

Purdue Hackers gets to exist at the scale we do because people believe in us.

Sometimes that means a company sponsors Hack Night. Sometimes it means a startup funds Microgrants. Sometimes it means a recruiter visits campus, an engineer gives a talk, or a founder helps students understand what it looks like to build something ambitious in the real world.

But none of that happens automatically. Someone has to find the right people, reach out in a way that feels specific and compelling, and help them understand why Purdue Hackers is worth supporting.

We’re looking for someone to run Sponsor Outreach and help turn promising leads into real conversations with companies.

This role is about cold emailing, creative outreach, sponsor conversations, follow-ups, and framing Purdue Hackers in a way that connects to what each company actually cares about. A developer tools startup, an AI lab, a hardware company, and a university recruiting team all care about different things. Your job is to understand that, tell the right version of the Purdue Hackers story, and help bring more resources and opportunities to our community.

You’ll work closely with the finance team and get direct mentorship from Ray on sponsor strategy, writing, positioning, and relationship-building. This is a great role for someone who wants to get good at sales, partnerships, fundraising, startups, or just explaining ambitious work to important people.

## What you will do

- Reach out to prospective sponsors, recruiting partners, alumni, founders, and company contacts
- Write thoughtful cold emails and follow-ups that make Purdue Hackers feel specific, credible, and exciting
- Tailor outreach based on each company’s goals, whether that is recruiting, brand awareness, developer adoption, student support, or community involvement
- Help pitch sponsorships for Hack Night, Microgrants, The Show, workshops, hardware projects, and other Purdue Hackers programs
- Take intro calls with sponsors and help move conversations toward clear next steps
- Work with prospect researchers to prioritize leads and understand who we should contact
- Track outreach, responses, follow-ups, and relationship status in our CRM
- Help improve our sponsorship messaging, pitch materials, email templates, and outreach systems

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Are a strong writer, or are seriously interested in becoming one
- Are comfortable reaching out to people you do not know
- Can think from a company’s perspective and understand what they might care about
- Are personable, persistent, and good at following up without being annoying
- Have good judgment about tone, timing, and how to make an ask feel natural
- Are excited to learn about sponsorships, fundraising, sales, partnerships, and relationship-building
- Want direct mentorship and are willing to iterate on your writing, calls, and strategy

Bonus points if you:

- Have experience with cold emails, sales, fundraising, sponsorships, recruiting, startups, or partnerships
- Are familiar with developer tools, AI companies, startups, university recruiting, or technical communities
- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
