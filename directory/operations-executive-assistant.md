# [Operations] Executive Assistant

Purdue Hackers is a lot to hold in one person’s head. There are events to run, people to check in with, sponsors to follow up with, projects to unblock, ideas to evaluate, messages to send, systems to improve, and a constant stream of tiny decisions that somehow all matter.

We’re looking for an Executive Assistant to work closely with the President to help run Purdue Hackers.

This is a special role for someone who cares deeply about the community and wants to help keep the whole machine moving. You’ll be a second brain, a sounding board, an accountability partner, and a trusted operator for the person leading Purdue Hackers. Some days, that means helping think through a big strategic decision. Other days, it means making sure a pile of small tasks actually gets finished.

This role is not about having a fancy title. It’s about being unusually responsible, thoughtful, proactive, and committed to making Purdue Hackers work better. You’ll get to see a lot of the behind-the-scenes context of how the organization runs, help connect dots across teams, and make sure important things do not fall through the cracks.

## What you will do

- Work closely with the President to help manage priorities, tasks, follow-ups, and ongoing projects
- Serve as a trusted thought partner for ideas, decisions, plans, and problems across Purdue Hackers
- Help break down vague or overwhelming projects into concrete next steps
- Track important deadlines, messages, meetings, and loose ends
- Follow up with organizers, team leads, partners, and community members when needed
- Help prepare for meetings, write notes, summarize decisions, and make sure next steps are clear
- Jump in on high-priority tasks when things start piling up
- Build lightweight systems that help the President and broader organizer team operate more smoothly

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Deeply resonate with Purdue Hackers and care a lot about making it better
- Are highly responsible, reliable, and good at following through
- Have strong judgment and can be trusted with sensitive context
- Are organized enough to keep track of many moving pieces without getting overwhelmed
- Are comfortable reminding people about things, asking clarifying questions, and pushing work forward
- Like being close to strategy, operations, people, and execution all at once
- Can switch between big-picture thinking and tiny logistical details
- Are the kind of person who notices when something is about to fall through the cracks and quietly catches it

Bonus points if you:

- Have helped run a club, project, team, event, startup, or community before
- Are good at writing, note-taking, planning, scheduling, or building organizational systems
- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
