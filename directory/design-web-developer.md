# [Design] Web Developer

Purdue Hackers ships a lot of websites. See [map.purduehackers.com](http://map.purduehackers.com) for a sample ;)

Some are simple landing pages for events. Some are weird interactive toys. Some are project pages, archives, applications, live dashboards, RSVP flows, or tiny internet objects that exist because someone had a funny idea at 2am.

We’re looking for a Web Developer to design and build websites for Purdue Hackers events, projects, and experiments. This role is about taking ideas from “we should make a site for this” to something real that people can visit, use, share, and remember.

You’ll work on websites for large events, internal tools, microsites, interactive experiences, and other web-based projects across Purdue Hackers. Some projects will be practical. Some will be beautiful. Some will be deeply unnecessary in the best way. The point is to make the internet feel like part of the Purdue Hackers universe!

Our stack is usually HTML, React, Astro, and Next.js, but we care more about shipping good work than using one specific framework. Use what you’re comfortable with, learn what you’re curious about, and bring your own taste to the things you build!

## What you will do

- Design and implement websites for Purdue Hackers events, programs, and projects
- Build landing pages for things like Hack Night, The Show, and special events
- Create interactive web experiences, archives, dashboards, application flows, and experimental internet things
- Work with designers, storytellers, and organizers to turn vague ideas into polished, useful websites
- Help maintain and improve existing Purdue Hackers web projects
- Build reusable components, templates, and systems so future sites are easier to make
- Bring your own technical and creative ideas to how Purdue Hackers shows up online

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Enjoy making websites that feel thoughtful, polished, and alive
- Are comfortable with HTML/CSS/JavaScript and at least one modern web framework
- Care about both how something works and how it feels to use
- Can take a rough idea and turn it into something real on the internet
- Like experimenting with interaction, motion, layout, typography, and strange little web details
- Are excited to own projects end-to-end, from concept to implementation to launch

Bonus points if you:

- Have experience with React, Astro, Next.js, Tailwind, TypeScript, or design tools like Figma
- Have built personal websites, event sites, interactive projects, or weird internet experiments before
- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
