# [Comms] Storyteller

Purdue Hackers is a home for students who build creative technical projects together. We run Hack Night every Friday, fund ambitious student projects through Microgrants, and put on The Show, our annual demo day where members share what they’ve built with hundreds of students, faculty, alumni, and sponsors.

But the work does not speak for itself automatically. Someone has to capture it, shape it, and tell the world why it matters.

We’re looking for a Storyteller to help document Purdue Hackers and communicate what makes this community special. This role is part writer, part editor, part community historian, part PR person, and part world-builder. You’ll help turn Hack Night projects, student stories, organizer work, sponsor updates, and random sparks of community magic into writing that makes people want to be part of Purdue Hackers.

Storytelling is how we make Purdue Hackers legible to students, sponsors, faculty, alumni, and ourselves. It is how we show people that this is more than a club; it is a place where ambitious, weird, curious people can find each other and make things together.

## What you will do

- Write our monthly VIP newsletter for sponsors, alumni, faculty, and other friends of Purdue Hackers
- Draft announcements for upcoming events, programs, and opportunities
- Interview members about their projects and turn those conversations into blog posts, spotlights, or short writeups
- Help document Hack Night, Microgrants, The Show, workshops, and other Purdue Hackers programs
- Work with organizers to explain what we are building, why it matters, and how people can get involved
- Help maintain the voice of Purdue Hackers across our website, blog, emails, Discord, and other public materials
- Build systems for regularly collecting stories from the community so our work does not disappear into the void

## About you

You will be a great fit for this role if you:

- Deeply resonate with Purdue Hackers and feel excited to help more people understand it
- Are a strong writer, or are seriously interested in becoming one
- Are curious about people and enjoy asking them about what they’re building
- Can take messy raw material and turn it into something clear, compelling, and polished

Bonus points if you:

- Are technical or willing to learn
- Are a natural leader & are willing to eventually lead a small team
- Are weird and/or have felt in the past like you struggled to find your people

Many of these are just suggestions; please apply if you are interested even if you don't feel like you tick every box.
